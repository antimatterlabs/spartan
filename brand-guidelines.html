LOGO + WAVE SYSTEM

Primary Logo

Use the full logo as the default brand signature. Keep clear space around it equal to the height of the logo mark and avoid placing it over busy image detail.

Logo Variants

Black logo belongs on white and light gray. White logo belongs on navy, charcoal, teal, or controlled photo overlays. Use the full lockup consistently across primary brand surfaces.

Wave Asset

Use For

Image masks, section dividers, category card bottoms, footer edges, and subtle background marks.

Avoid

Do not use it under every heading, as a logo replacement, or so large that it feels decorative and playful.

Do Not

Do not stretch, rotate, recolor outside the palette, add shadows, outline, or crowd the primary logo.
